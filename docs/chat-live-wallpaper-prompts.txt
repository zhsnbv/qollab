ЖИВЫЕ ФОНЫ · ЦВЕТ И СВЕТ

Пять самостоятельных промптов для генерации видео. Начать лучше с «Северного шёлка»: он сочетает насыщенность северного сияния с понравившимся мягким движением шёлка. Здесь нужны заметные цветовые поля и глубина цвета, а не бледная почти однотонная заливка. Чётких предметов, контуров и отдельных фигур быть не должно.

ПАРАМЕТРЫ
Вертикальный формат 9:16, 8–10 секунд, 24 или 30 кадров/с, без звука. Неподвижная камера. Если доступны одинаковые начальный и конечный кадры, использовать один кадр для обоих. Движение плавное, периодическое; длительность цикла — весь ролик. Для первого отбора достаточно по 2 варианта выбранного промпта.

01 · Северный шёлк — первый выбор

Create a 10-second vertical 9:16 abstract animated wallpaper for a mobile chat. Fill the entire frame with rich, luminous color fields inspired by the light of an aurora and the fluid sheen of silk, without depicting an actual sky or fabric. Saturated emerald and turquoise dissolve into deep cobalt and luminous violet, with broad translucent overlaps that create a sense of volume inside the color. Make the hues clearly present and expressive, not washed-out pastels or an almost uniform pale fill. Every transition is deeply diffused: soft gradients merging into gradients, no visible boundaries, outlined shapes, ribbons, spheres or discrete glowing blobs. Use large-scale color variation and restrained luminance contrast, with no bright hotspot or empty central area. Animate a slow continuous circulation of the light fields, like color gently turning within itself. The movement should be visible but calm, with one complete 10-second cycle returning toward the same color distribution and motion state. Locked camera, constant exposure, no zoom or cuts. No horizon, landscape, stars, particles, bokeh circles, textures, objects, text, logos or UI. A seamless-loop candidate with stable saturated color and perfectly smooth motion; prioritize an unobtrusive join for later verification.

02 · Малиновый шёлк

Create a 10-second vertical 9:16 animated color-field abstraction for a messaging wallpaper. Build a full-frame atmosphere of saturated raspberry, warm coral and red-violet, flowing through translucent rose and a restrained amber glow. Color should feel deep, dimensional and luminous from within, with expressive midtones; avoid pale pink wash, beige dominance and white empty space. Borrow only the sensation of light moving across silk, never depict cloth, folds or material edges. All color masses merge through broad, strongly blurred gradients. No individual objects, geometric silhouettes, isolated blobs or hard boundaries. Keep detail extremely low-frequency so chat bubbles remain visually distinct. Animate a gentle periodic redistribution of the warm and cool light: the raspberry region slowly opens into coral while violet softly moves through it, then returns to the initial balance over one complete 10-second cycle. The camera is completely fixed, exposure and overall brightness remain stable, no flashes or pulsation. No ribbons, spheres, petals, horizon, scenery, stars, particles, bokeh, grain, text, logos or interface. Produce a calm continuous loop candidate, aiming for closely matched beginning and ending color fields and velocity.

03 · Кобальтовое свечение

Create a 10-second vertical 9:16 abstract animated wallpaper composed entirely of softly diffused light and color. Use vivid cobalt blue, electric periwinkle and deep amethyst, with broad cyan illumination passing through the overlapping gradients. The palette is saturated and unmistakably colorful, with dimensional translucent depth rather than a flat pale wash. Maintain moderate brightness: no black voids, white glare or intense neon hotspots. The entire composition is a continuous field with no discernible foreground objects, edges, circles or separate shapes. Think of colored light seen through layers of heavily defocused atmosphere, without visible fog texture or a physical surface. Animate an unhurried rotational flow inside the color distribution, a slow elegant change that remains perceptible at phone size. Complete one smooth 10-second circulation, returning toward the first color arrangement without stopping or reversing abruptly. Static camera, constant exposure, no parallax, no cuts. No actual aurora curtains, sky, horizon, stars, particles, bokeh, lens flares, grids, stripes, contour lines, text, logos or UI. Create a seamless-loop candidate whose visual interest comes from luminous color volume and soft movement alone.

04 · Нефритовый свет

Create a 10-second vertical 9:16 animated abstract wallpaper with rich jade, warm sage and luminous turquoise color fields. Add a restrained golden-green illumination within the gradients, creating a fresh, warm-cool balance. The greens and aquas must be clearly visible and saturated, not desaturated grey-green or an almost white background. Create depth through large translucent overlaps of color, with every boundary dissolved into an exceptionally soft blur. No recognizable shapes, outlined layers, leaves, water, waves, glass or physical materials. Fill the frame continuously; keep luminance variation gentle while allowing strong hue differences. Animate a slow internal tidal flow of color without drawing a visible tide or wave: turquoise gently moves through jade, the golden-green light diffuses across it, and the fields naturally return to their starting balance during one complete cycle. Smooth constant movement, no sudden acceleration, brightness pulse or abrupt reversal. Locked camera, no zoom, cuts or exposure changes. No objects, horizon, landscape, stars, particles, bokeh circles, texture, text, logos or interface. A calm 10-second loop candidate with a discreet join to be inspected after generation.

05 · Спектральная глубина

Create a 10-second vertical 9:16 abstract animated wallpaper with a sophisticated, vividly colored atmosphere. Broad fields of cyan, iris violet and coral overlap through translucent magenta and deep blue. Use a carefully controlled spectral palette: several clearly visible colors with smooth dimensional blending, not a conventional rainbow, pastel mesh wash or bright multicolor stripes. No hue is enclosed in a separate blob; the entire frame behaves as one continuous luminous volume. Strongly blur every transition until there are no visible contours, shapes, surfaces or individual elements. Keep the image rich in color but quiet in luminance, with no black areas, white flares or singular focal point. Animate a slow continuous migration of the overlapping light fields along a closed motion cycle, revealing subtle new color mixtures without adding any objects. Over 10 seconds, return toward the initial distribution and direction of flow. Fixed camera and constant exposure; no scene changes, zoom, jolts or flicker. No spheres, ribbons, fabric, aurora curtains, horizon, stars, particles, bokeh, fine texture, text, logos or UI. Produce a smooth immersive loop candidate intended for verification and final loop finishing.

ПОСЛЕ ГЕНЕРАЦИИ
Пришлите исходные MP4/MOV без повторного сжатия мессенджером и без самостоятельной обрезки. Проверим, что цвет достаточно выразительный, границы форм не проявляются в движении, а сообщения хорошо читаются в светлой и тёмной теме.
Затем сравним начало и конец по цвету, свету и скорости движения. При необходимости выберем удачный участок или сделаем короткое перекрытие; заметное двоение или сильное расхождение лучше исправлять новой генерацией. Одинаковый стартовый и финальный кадр помогает, но идеальную бесшовность текстовый промпт не гарантирует.
После выбора подготовим зацикленный бесшумный файл и статичный постер, подберём разрешение и сжатие по фактическому качеству. Проверим плавность градиентов после сжатия, чтобы не появились полосы. Для ограничения движения используется постер, вне видимого чата воспроизведение ставится на паузу.
